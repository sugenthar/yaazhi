# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Yaazhi a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender,
experience level, nationality, or religion.

## Our standards

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive; welcome Tamil- and English-speakers alike.
- Accept feedback gracefully; focus on the code and the facts.
- No harassment, insults, threats, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported via GitHub Issues on the source repository.
Maintainers will review, warn or remove offenders, and keep reports
confidential to the extent possible.
